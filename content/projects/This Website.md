This site is the home of Simons Development Labs, my independent creative practice. Work, Studio, and Notes bring together software, objects, experiments, and writing.

It's built with Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and small Preact components. Most pages are generated as static HTML. Markdown holds the project writing, and ordinary hyperlinks connect published pages.

The earlier version used Nuxt Content and an Obsidian vault. I kept the existing project URLs while simplifying how I write for the site.
