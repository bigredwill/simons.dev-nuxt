I’m currently working through STUDIO WORKS, an experimental studio incubator for artists and creative technologists developing meaningful, commercially viable objects.

Over ten weeks, we move from idea to prototype to production and sale. This week begins with a question: what should I make?

I’m looking back through the things I’ve already made. The light built from a found post. The printed pages. The tools for exploring skateboarding’s history. Which ideas still have energy? Which ones would I want to keep making?

For now, the work is to notice what keeps coming back.
